## What we collect, and why

**When you create an account.** You sign in with Google or Apple. They tell us
your name and email address, and we store those so we know who you are and can
reach you about your rides. We never see or receive your Google or Apple
password.

**When you pay.** Payments go through Paystack. They collect your mobile money or
card details in their own checkout, and those details never reach us. What comes
back to us is a payment reference, the amount, and your phone number, which we
keep so we can identify your account and contact you about a payment.

**When you ride.** We store which days you said you would travel, in which
direction, and which stop you board and leave at. When you board we record the
scan, the driver who scanned it, and whether it succeeded. This is what settles a
dispute if you are told you already boarded when you did not.

**Your photo, if you add one.** The driver sees it on the manifest so they can
match a face to a booked seat when a QR code will not scan.

**Your device, so we can reach you.** We store a push notification token so we
can send you the daily "are you travelling tomorrow" question and tell you when
your van is close.

**Diagnostics.** Our apps use Firebase to record crashes and basic usage, which
tells us where the app breaks and where people get stuck. This includes your
device model, operating system version, app version, and an identifier Firebase
assigns to your installation.

## What we do not collect

**We do not track where you are.** The rider app never sends us your location.
The live map works by showing you where the _van_ is, using GPS reported by the
driver's phone during a trip. That data is attached to the trip, not to you, and
there is no record anywhere in our systems of where any rider has been.

**We never hold your payment details.** No card number, no mobile money PIN.

**We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not share it with
advertisers.

## Who else sees your information

We share only what each of these needs to do its job:

- **Paystack**, to take your payment
- **Google** and **Apple**, to confirm you are who you say when you sign in
- **Firebase**, to deliver notifications and record crashes
- **Render** and **Cloudflare**, who host our systems and store your photo
- **Your driver**, who sees your name, your photo, and your booked stop on the
  manifest for the trip you booked

We will also disclose information where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open.

Records of what you paid are kept for [6] years after the payment, because we are
required to keep financial records. Boarding records are kept for [2] years so we
can settle a dispute. Vehicle GPS from a trip is kept for [90] days.

When you delete your account, see below.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app, under Profile. You do not need
to email anyone or wait for us to approve it.

When you do:

- You are signed out everywhere immediately and we stop sending you notifications
- If you signed in with Apple, we tell Apple to revoke our access
- Your name, email, phone number, and photo are erased, and the photo is deleted
  from storage
- Signing in again creates a completely new account. The old one cannot be
  recovered, by you or by us

**One thing survives, and we would rather explain it than hide it.** We keep the
record of what you paid and what you were charged, with your name and every other
identifying detail stripped out. We are required to keep financial records, and
what remains is a meaningless account number attached to a list of amounts. It
cannot be connected back to you.

## Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you have the right to ask us
what personal data we hold about you, to correct it if it is wrong, to ask us to
delete it, to object to how we are using it, and to complain to the Data
Protection Commission.

Ask us by emailing [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. We will respond within [30] days. For
deletion, the in-app button is faster than emailing us.

## Children

Trotxi is not intended for children under [16]. We do not knowingly collect their
information. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we
will remove it.

## Keeping it safe

Your session is protected by a token stored securely on your device, never a
password we hold. Those tokens are stored on our side only as a one-way hash, so
even we cannot read them. Your boarding code is stored the same way. Traffic
between the app and our servers is encrypted.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach
affects your personal data we will notify you and the Data Protection Commission
as the law requires.
