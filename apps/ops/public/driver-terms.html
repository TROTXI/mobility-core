The short version

  • Use only your own driver code and PIN, and never share them.
  • The app shares your location only during a trip you start, and stops when the trip ends or you sign out.
  • Never use the app in a way that distracts you while the vehicle is moving.
  • Mark riders boarded or absent only when that is what happened.
  • Use rider details only to run the trip, and never keep or share them.

1. About these Terms

These Terms apply when you use the Trotxi Driver app, provided by Trotxi Limited Company (company registration number CS164310826, Data Protection Commission certificate PROV2609473). They cover your use of the app only.

They do not create, replace or change any employment, contractor, vehicle or transport-operator agreement you have with Trotxi or a transport partner. If these Terms conflict with that agreement about your work, the agreement applies.

2. Access and sign-in

Trotxi operations creates your driver account and gives you a driver code. You choose a private PIN. Our staff cannot see your PIN. Setup and PIN-reset instructions are sent by email or SMS to the contact details operations holds for you, so tell operations when they change.

  • Use only your own driver code and PIN, and never share them.
  • Never let anyone else drive a trip under your account.
  • If you sign in on a shared or company phone, sign out at the end of your shift.
  • Report a lost or stolen phone to operations at once so we can end the session.

You may use the app only while operations authorises you. Access ends when your authorisation ends, and may be paused while we look into a safety, security or conduct concern.

3. Driving safely

Safety comes before the app. Do not read the screen, scan passes or reply to messages while the vehicle is moving: stop safely first. Follow road-safety law and dispatch instructions, and keep your licence and vehicle documents current as operations requires.

4. Trips and location sharing

When you start an assigned trip with the app open, the app begins sharing your phone's location for that trip. Riders booked on the trip see the bus on their map, and operations sees it for dispatch and safety.

  • Sharing continues with the screen locked or the app in the background, for that trip only.
  • Android shows a notification while sharing is on; iOS may show its location indicator.
  • Sharing stops when you end the trip, sign out or your assignment ends.
  • Without signal, the app keeps locations on your phone and sends them when you reconnect.

Keep the app running and your phone charged during trips. Report stop arrivals and trip progress honestly. Never send false or altered location information, or try to make a trip look as if it ran when it did not.

Our Privacy Notice explains exactly what location information we keep and for how long.

5. Boarding and rider details

Check each rider against your trip list by scanning their boarding pass or entering their boarding code. Mark a rider as boarded or absent only when that is what happened. A wrong mark can cost a rider a paid ride.

Your trip list shows riders' names, photos and stops so you can board them. Use these details only to run the trip. Do not copy, photograph, share or keep them, and do not contact riders privately.

6. Incidents

Report incidents, collisions, unsafe conditions and passenger-safety concerns through the app or to operations as soon as it is safe. Describe what you saw. In an emergency, contact the emergency services first: the app is not an emergency service.

7. Ratings

Riders can rate rides from 1 to 5 stars. Operations may review ratings together with trip records to improve the service.

8. Proper use

Use the app only for your Trotxi work. Do not interfere with dispatch, boarding, payments or tracking, look at information you are not assigned, or try to access any Trotxi system you are not allowed to. Trotxi owns the app and its content; maps, data and open-source software keep their own licences.

9. Your account and your information

Our Privacy Notice explains what the app collects and your rights. To close your driver account, contact operations or follow our account deletion steps. Closing the app account does not end any separate agreement you have about your work.

10. Changes and law

When we publish a new version of these Terms, the app will show it to you and ask you to accept it before you continue. You also accept these Terms in the app before your first trip. These Terms are governed by the laws of Ghana and do not take away any right you have under Ghana law.
