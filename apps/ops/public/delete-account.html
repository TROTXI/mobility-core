Trotxi Commuter & Trotxi Driver

Leave Trotxi. Request deletion.

You can request account and associated personal-data deletion even if you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in. A request is not an instant deletion: we verify identity first and explain any information that must remain.

Send a deletion request

Email privacy@trotxi.com to request account deletion, deletion of particular data, or help with a privacy question. You do not need to sign in or reinstall the app.

Sending an email starts a human-reviewed request; it does not automatically delete your account.

What to include

  • Say whether you use Trotxi Commuter or Trotxi Driver.
  • Give your account name and sign-in email; for a driver-code account, give your driver code and contact email or phone number.
  • Say whether you want your whole account deleted or are requesting deletion of particular data.

Do not send your PIN, password, access tokens, full card number or card security code. Knowing a driver code or an email address alone does not authorise deletion. We may ask you to prove ownership through an appropriate verified account or operations-assisted check.

Already signed in as a commuter?

Open Profile → Security & sign-in → Delete account and confirm. This uses the existing authenticated account-erasure workflow. The email request route is also available and does not require reinstalling the app.

Driver requests

Drivers can use the same public request route, or ask operations. Driver accounts are provisioned by operations, so requests are reviewed and ownership is verified before account erasure. Suspending a credential, resetting a PIN or archiving a driver is not the same as deleting personal information.

What happens after verification

  1. We confirm the account and the scope of your request. An email being composed or sent does not mean it has been reviewed or approved.
  2. Account erasure disables access and revokes sessions and device registrations. Profile identifiers and sign-in links are scrubbed; driver contact and licence details are also scrubbed when the linked driver account is erased.
  3. Membership assignments and applicable future reservations are closed or released. Private-photo removal and applicable external provider cleanup may finish later through retryable maintenance tasks.
  4. We explain any retained records and outstanding cleanup. We do not treat a failed external cleanup attempt as completed erasure.

Deletion is irreversible. It does not delete your Google or Apple account. A deletion request is not automatically a refund request: tell us separately if there is an outstanding payment or refund concern. We do not require you to buy another subscription to request deletion.

Information that may remain

Payment and accounting entries, settled boarding/trip history, audit and security records, and relevant incident evidence may remain for reconciliation, disputes, safety, fraud prevention or applicable legal obligations. Retained records are not a licence to keep using a deleted account for ordinary service.

Raw driver GPS traces are separately eligible for deletion after 30 days, except evidence windows under an active hold. Retention sweeps and external-file cleanup must run before physical deletion is complete. We will explain the relevant remaining data and retention basis when handling your request; we do not promise one universal deletion period for every record.

Response and follow-up

This pilot uses a monitored, human-reviewed request route, not an automated deletion form. Ask us for progress using the same email thread. We cannot publish a guaranteed completion time for an unverified request or promise immediate removal from every external system.
